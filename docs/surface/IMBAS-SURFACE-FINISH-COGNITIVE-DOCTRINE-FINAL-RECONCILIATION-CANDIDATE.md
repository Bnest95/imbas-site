# 0. Reconciliation verdict

Claude's adversarial pass materially improved the 20-rule synthesis. Most of its restorations are accepted.

Four corrections are made before freeze:

1. **R14 accent registry:** the registry must be seeded from the actual standing accent law. A behavior-class label is not automatically entitled to ember merely because the live surface currently uses it.
2. **R15 behavior-label enforcement:** size/weight can be structurally constrained; a numeric contrast-ratio ordering is not used as a proxy for "non-accusatory," because a ruled accent role may legitimately have higher chromatic contrast while remaining subordinate. Tone remains a Gate-2 question.
3. **R19 zero-findings template:** zero-findings preserves the same result scaffold and typographic hierarchy but does not fabricate empty finding/action slots. Nonexistent finding/action regions collapse with zero seam, consistent with Actionability's empty contract.
4. **R21 accessible mark relation:** the mark→explanation relationship must be programmatically determinable and keyboard-operable as a path, but doctrine does not require every inline mark itself to become a separate tab stop. The exact annotation/noteref interaction pattern remains an implementation/accessibility decision.

With these corrections, the doctrine is ready to freeze after current-master reproduction of live-intake items.

# 1. Executive thesis

The Reader minimizes **reconstruction cost**.

The intended sequence is:

**ORIENT → RECOGNIZE THE FINDING → CONNECT IT TO EVIDENCE → KNOW THE NEXT QUESTION → INSPECT DEEPER IF NEEDED**

The product does not optimize for minimalism, maximal trust, or engagement. It optimizes for correct comprehension, calibrated trust, inspectability, agency, and low working-memory demand.

Visual salience communicates structure, not guilt or certainty. GLANCE establishes the frame; READ reconnects the finding to the user's own answer; INSPECT satisfies scrutiny.

# 2. Final binding doctrine — 22 rules

## R1 — One clear orientation winner in GLANCE
One result-level element clearly owns first-pass orientation. Count, taxonomy, evidence, provenance and CTA do not compete as peer headlines.
- **Evidence:** established hierarchy/cognitive-accessibility guidance; visual-complexity evidence.
- **Enforcement:** structural hierarchy now; actual orientation by Gate 2.
- **Prevents:** dashboard-like first screen.

## R2 — `blocks_before` is a governed ratchet, not a cognitive law
No change may increase `blocks_before` for any finding class without a recorded founder ruling. Any numeric ceiling belongs to the instrument/configuration, not to psychology.
- **Evidence:** progressive-disclosure support + existing Imbas measurement.
- **Enforcement:** MEASURABLE-NOW.
- **Prevents:** pre-evidence explanation creep.

## R3 — A finding's evidence unit is locally grouped; deeper disclosure stays in context
Each finding's label, explanation and quoted anchor live in one existing finding container/region with no unrelated content interleaved. This rule does **not** require new card chrome. A finding-level action, where a real producer exists, stays inside that finding region; no action is manufactured to complete the unit. Deeper disclosure does not navigate away from or cover the triggering evidence.
- **Evidence:** proximity/common-region, recognition-over-recall, spatial contiguity.
- **Enforcement:** DOM/container/order checks; Gate 2 for comprehension.
- **Prevents:** reconstruction burden and card-creep-by-doctrine.

## R4 — QUOTED reconnects to the actual passage; mark numerals follow answer order
A QUOTED finding reconnects to the marked passage in the person's answer; a detached summary is never the only proof path. Mark numerals ascend in document order and are assigned after resolution. Any separately sorted findings list still displays the answer-order numeral.
- **Evidence:** recognition, spatial contiguity, working-memory burden.
- **Enforcement:** MEASURABLE-NOW.
- **Prevents:** dual-order mapping tax.

## R5 — ABSENT is first-class and non-positional
ABSENT may carry equal conceptual weight to QUOTED but never receives a fabricated span, offset, caret, or inline mark. It remains record-level.
- **Evidence:** product-law constrained extrapolation.
- **Enforcement:** MEASURABLE-NOW.
- **Prevents:** false precision.

## R6 — UNRESOLVED leaves zero residue
UNRESOLVED cannot create a visible mark, action, count artifact, placeholder, spacing seam, focus stop, hidden control, numeral gap, or exported indication.
- **Evidence:** standing Imbas doctrine + consistency/accessibility.
- **Enforcement:** negative-control DOM/export diff.
- **Prevents:** evidence failure leaking into interpretation.

## R7 — At most one primary-emphasis control is co-visible
Within a single viewport at any scroll position, at most one result-surface control may carry primary visual emphasis. Non-co-visible contexts may each have a primary. Co-visible peers are subordinate and differentiated.
- **Evidence:** visual guidance/highlighting scarcity + action hierarchy.
- **Enforcement:** viewport/co-visibility check.
- **Prevents:** two simultaneous primaries and CTA parity.

## R8 — Action follows its producing content structurally
Finding-level actions render after the finding content they derive from and stay inside the finding region. Run-level actions render after the relevant run-level interpretive content. Sticky/persistent action UI defaults to no and requires demonstrated need. Whether the user actually comprehends before acting is a Gate-2 question.
- **Evidence:** task continuity, progressive commitment, information scent.
- **Enforcement:** DOM order/placement now; comprehension via probe.
- **Prevents:** premature persuasion and post-comprehension hunting.

## R9 — Action labels predict the operation
Action labels communicate what will happen — copy a question, inspect evidence, compare treatment, etc. — rather than promise a substantive outcome.
- **Evidence:** information scent / information foraging.
- **Enforcement:** Gate-2 prediction probe.
- **Prevents:** generic AI-wrapper mental model.

## R10 — Give-nothing-away governs visual framing and agency
A neutral next question cannot be surrounded by UI that implies the expected answer. The derived question renders as literal, selectable final text before any control that acts on it. No probe gates it. No control auto-sends it or generates the substantive answer.
- **Evidence:** framing/confirmation-bias literature + standing product law.
- **Enforcement:** DOM order, network-path assertions, human expectation probe.
- **Prevents:** steering and producer-boundary collapse.

## R11 — Provenance is inspectable but structurally subordinate
Inspectability is visibly signaled. Heavy provenance artifacts — hashes, record IDs, custody data — never precede the first finding. Export/share/receipt controls render after the last interpretive block of their stratum, never between interpretive blocks. A compact source/time cue may appear earlier only when it directly aids interpretation and is ruled in the implementation brief. Methodological depth stays INSPECT-level.
- **Evidence:** provenance/trust-calibration research; nonprobative-detail effects.
- **Enforcement:** DOM order + Gate-2 discoverability.
- **Prevents:** authority theater and compliance-console hierarchy.

## R12 — Optimize calibrated trust, not maximum trust
Success is a correct mental model of what Imbas observed and did not establish. A higher self-reported trust score is not an acceptance target.
- **Evidence:** strong automation/human-AI trust literature.
- **Enforcement:** Gate-2 mental-model/reliance tasks.
- **Prevents:** trust-maximizing dark epistemics.

## R13 — Scope/non-verdict language is present but not dominant
At most one **full** scope statement renders per stratum, located at the stratum boundary, in normal body register without warning/alert treatment. Short references may point back to it. Whether people actually understand the boundary is tested, not inferred from rendering.
- **Evidence:** uncertainty communication + habituation/attention principles.
- **Enforcement:** structural count/style checks + Gate 2.
- **Prevents:** disclaimer wallpaper and hidden boundary.

## R14 — Ember spends only against the standing role registry
Every ember use must map to a role already authorized by the standing accent law. The seed registry is:
1. evidence signal/mark, including its numeral;
2. one ruled keyword within a heading where explicitly authorized;
3. a consequential gap/figure where explicitly ruled;
4. a mono/status pill where appropriate and ruled;
5. the primary CTA/action.

A behavior-class label is **not automatically entitled** to ember; if current product law separately authorizes it as the signal, that authorization must be recorded explicitly. New ember roles require founder ruling. No numeric ceiling is claimed by research.
- **Evidence:** salience/preattentive guidance and highlighting-frequency evidence + locked aesthetic doctrine.
- **Enforcement:** accent-role census against registry.
- **Prevents:** role-registry gaming and salience inflation.

## R15 — Findings never use prosecution, grading, danger, or false-confidence grammar
No warning glyphs, danger tokens, strikethrough-like marks, severity iconography, "caught" treatment, confidence scores, severity levels, or percentages on individual findings unless such a quantity becomes a separately validated governed measurement. Behavior-class labels may not outrank the finding explanation by size or font weight. Their perceived tone is a Gate-2 question; no contrast-ratio inequality is used as a proxy for feeling.
- **Evidence:** framing/reactance/confirmation-bias evidence.
- **Enforcement:** structural token/type checks + Gate-2 tone probe.
- **Prevents:** visual prosecution and pseudo-certification.

## R16 — 390px defaults to a single-column evidence narrative
At 390px, orientation → finding → evidence → action → deeper provenance remains understandable by vertical scroll alone. Side-by-side layout is permitted only when it causes no tiny text, horizontal scroll, or cross-column association requirement for binding finding to evidence/action.
- **Evidence:** narrow-screen search/memory/accessibility rationale.
- **Enforcement:** responsive structural tests + Gate 2.
- **Prevents:** mobile comparison-memory tax.

## R17 — Accessibility floors are binding
Meet WCAG 2.2 target-size, focus-order, focus-visibility/not-obscured, reflow, text-spacing and relevant relationship requirements. An inline mark/number may rely on the inline-target exception only while an equivalent same-finding control exists as required by the standard. Content survives text-spacing overrides and 400% zoom/reflow without loss of meaning or function.
- **Evidence:** normative accessibility standards + motor-control evidence.
- **Enforcement:** MEASURABLE-NOW.
- **Prevents:** inaccessible evidence and action flow.

## R18 — Feedback is local; no autonomous post-completion spectacle
Copy/expand/handoff feedback is immediate and local, without focus steal or page jump. After result completion, no element autonomously counts, pulses, staggers, reveals, or animates without user input. Input-triggered feedback remains permitted and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- **Evidence:** system-status guidance + preattentive motion evidence + product register.
- **Enforcement:** no-input frame diff + focus/state assertions.
- **Prevents:** reveal/gotcha grammar and notification noise.

## R19 — Zero-findings preserves the same epistemic scaffold without fabricating empty UI
A zero-finding result uses the same overall result scaffold, typography hierarchy, boundary treatment, and record posture as a populated result. It does **not** fabricate empty finding blocks, disabled actions, or spacing shells; nonexistent finding/action regions collapse with zero seam. No success color, checkmark, "clean/verified/passed/complete" grammar, or celebratory treatment appears.
- **Evidence:** implied-truth effect + standing empty-action law.
- **Enforcement:** scaffold/style diff + zero-seam assertions + Gate-2 interpretation.
- **Prevents:** false certification while preserving honest emptiness.

## R20 — Density may increase only when the density is evidence
GLANCE does not become metadata-dense. READ and INSPECT may be dense where the density consists of evidence that lowers verification cost — not decorative scaffolding, duplicated prose, provenance theater, or metadata volume. No universal item-count ceiling exists. Feature variety (type sizes, accent roles/values, border/enclosure treatments) is the relevant governed variable; any exact ceiling is founder-owned.
- **Evidence:** visual-complexity/clutter literature.
- **Enforcement:** feature-variety audit + judgment.
- **Prevents:** dashboard/compliance density disguised as rigor.

## R21 — Evidence marks preserve legibility and an accessible path to explanation
Evidence marks preserve ordinary readable ink and meet at least WCAG AA text contrast after compositing. The current low-alpha tint treatment is an accepted baseline; doctrine does not claim it is the only possible compliant visual treatment. Every mark has a programmatically determinable relationship to its explanation, and keyboard/screen-reader users have an operable path between the evidence and explanation. The exact noteref/annotation interaction pattern is not frozen, and every inline mark need not become an individual tab stop if an equivalent accessible mechanism is provided.
- **Evidence:** WCAG contrast/relationships + live measured contrast.
- **Enforcement:** composited contrast; relationship/accessibility tests.
- **Prevents:** evidence disappearing for low-vision, keyboard, or screen-reader users.

## R22 — One canonical full statement per finding; other appearances must have a distinct named job
Each finding has one canonical full statement in the result experience. Other appearances may only:
- orient/compress the finding without independently restating it;
- point to the canonical statement/evidence;
- support the handoff/action without introducing a second independent paraphrase.

Any additional full paraphrased statement requires explicit justification in the implementation brief.
- **Evidence:** redundancy/split-attention evidence, narrowed to avoid overclaim.
- **Enforcement:** content-role audit; snapshot/string inventory.
- **Prevents:** four-way paraphrase drift and repeated cognitive re-parsing.

# 3. Gate 2 — final task set

## G1 — Orientation
**Prompt:** "What happened here?"
**Success:** user identifies the observation about the AI answer, not only the topic or methodology.
**Diagnoses:** GLANCE hierarchy/orientation.

## G2 — Claim→proof linkage
**Prompt:** "Show me what part of the answer this finding is connected to."
**Success:** correct passage without trial-and-error; record scroll/backtrack behavior.
**Diagnoses:** mark numbering, proximity, relationship.

## G3 — ABSENT + mark grammar
**Prompt:** "For each of these findings, show me where in the original answer it applies. If it isn't present in the answer, say so."
**Success:** QUOTED is located; ABSENT is understood as non-present without hunting for a fake mark.
**Rider:** ask what an inline mark means. Failure if a mark on adjacent text is interpreted as "this sentence is false/wrong" when the finding concerns missing context.
**Diagnoses:** ABSENT mental model and dual mark grammar.

## G4 — Action information scent
**Before activation:** "What do you think this will do?"
**Success:** predicts next-question/copy/handoff behavior, not "fix the answer."
**Diagnoses:** action label, timing, hierarchy.

## G5 — Non-verdict / zero-findings
**Prompt:** "What does this tell you about the answer? Does it mean the answer is correct or verified?"
**Success:** no surfaced finding is not read as certification.
**Diagnoses:** implied-truth effect, boundary failure.

## G6 — Register/tone
**Prompt:** "What is this interface saying about the AI answer? Does it feel like it's pointing something out, or judging/catching the model?"
**Success:** observational/inspection framing rather than accusation/gotcha.
**Diagnoses:** label prominence, accent allocation, mark grammar.

# 4. 390px hard checks

- no horizontal overflow;
- semantic reading order preserved;
- long URLs/hashes/tokens wrap;
- finding/evidence/action remain locally ordered;
- WCAG target/focus rules pass;
- no focus obscuration;
- text-spacing override survives;
- 400% zoom / ~320 CSS px reflow survives;
- evidence mark remains legible when wrapping across lines;
- composited mark contrast passes;
- no UNRESOLVED DOM/focus seam;
- no side-by-side comparison requiring horizontal scanning or tiny type;
- long next-question text wraps;
- mark→explanation accessible relationship remains operable.

No universal line-length, screens-to-action, or mark-distance threshold is claimed.

# 7. Implementation risks

1. **Primary-per-viewport inflation:** R7 is a ceiling, not permission to place a primary in every viewport.
2. **Accent-role gaming:** new ember roles require founder ruling; implementers cannot rename decoration as semantics.
3. **Verification-density abuse:** R20 does not license hashes/IDs/metadata in READ merely because they look rigorous.
4. **Empty-state novelty:** R19 preserves the scaffold but also preserves zero-seam absence; do not create a special celebratory empty state or fake empty slots.
5. **Common-region card creep:** R3 uses the existing finding container/region; it does not authorize a stack of bordered SaaS cards.
6. **Accessibility overcorrection:** R21 requires an operable relationship, not necessarily one tab stop for every inline mark.
7. **Canonical-statement overcorrection:** R22 does not forbid brief orientation or handoff references; it forbids unnecessary independent full paraphrases.
