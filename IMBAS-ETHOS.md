## The surfaces

Things can be more than one thing. These are the things.

### The Reader

**Origin.** The live instrument: point it at your own AI answer and see what it left out, how it was framed, what it stepped around.

**For.** *(tree: IMBAS-CANON.md)*:

> "Not another model to trust. A way to check the ones you already use."

**Today.** Live at imbaslabs.com. Captures every public run into the pipeline automatically; review and publication are separate manual steps. Its estimates are unvalidated, and the receipt says so:

> "Reader inspections are discovery, not evidence. Nothing enters the Imbas record without protocol capture and a recorded human review." *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08; tree: reader-receipt.js, whitepaper.html, how-it-works.html, calibration.html, exploration-pack.html, volunteer-gap-paper.html)*

### Input Integrity

**Origin.** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*:

> "a cool feature that captures ai things not just the basic shit"

**For.** Reading what a PDF instructs, not just what it renders — *(tree: input-integrity.html)*: "What the file carries under the page." The file stays in the person's custody: "Your browser reads the file. The file does not leave this machine."

**Today.** Live. It reports what the file's structure instructs, "with the evidence beside every statement," and "reaches no verdict on the document or its author" *(tree: input-integrity.html)*.

### Corrective steering

**Origin.** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*:

> "easy low hanging fruit value add for people to wrestle an unruly ai"

**For.** Handing the reader something to do about what the inspection found: ready-made correction prompts drawn from a sealed, versioned bank.

**Today.** Live as the chip lane on the Reader result — two doors, "Something's off." and "Make it better." — with admission to the bank by founder ruling.

### The substrate and the record

**Origin.** The human-scored archive behind the Reader: observations become an independent record no single lab owns.

**For.** *(tree: IMBAS-CANON.md)*:

> "Imbas does not only preserve AI answers. It builds the record that can make the inspection layer smarter."

> "Over time, that validated record can become the training substrate for a specialized inspection agent: not another model to trust, but a system trained to notice what AI answers surface, miss, and reframe."

**Today.** The two-tier model operates: workbench → Repository pool → human-promoted Cases. Five cases have public pages, and that is the only archive count the tree establishes. The trained inspection agent is future tense — upside, not the pitch.

### The public record and the answer-layer lane

**Origin.** *(tree: IMBAS-CANON.md)*:

> "A public record keeps decisions anchored."

> "What gets measured gets better. A public record of what AI surfaces — and what it omits — keeps the decisions built on it anchored to what’s true, instead of drifting with the model."

**For.** How the record wins in the answer layer *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*:

> "You win by ruthlessly exposing undeniable drift, facts, manipulation and inconsistency. Accusation lets them play victim and claim conspiracy. Better info is how you win."

**Today.** The public record is live: the case archive, the papers, the field notes. The Observatory is a founder framing for where this lane goes; it is not yet built. The register the tree holds *(tree: whitepaper.html)*: "Imbas exists so the independent class exists," and "Time is part of the instrument."

### Professional use

**Origin.** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*:

> "AI is entering workflows governed by external professional standards; the obligations have not changed."

**For.** People whose work answers to standards outside the AI — "Compliance professionals in regulated industries" among them *(tree: institutions.html)* — who need what the record is: "Documented, citable, inspectable." *(tree: institutions.html)*

**Today.** The institutions surface is live and holds its restraints in writing: "Imbas does not infer model intent." "It does not certify model safety." *(tree: institutions.html)*

### Advisory

**Origin.** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*:

> "Human judgment first. AI where it creates real leverage."

> "Common sense before automation." *(also tree: advisory.html)*

> "AI is changing both sides of the business at once: how customers find you, and how the work gets done once they do." *(also tree: advisory.html)*

**For.** *(tree: advisory.html)*:

> "The goal isn’t to automate everything. It’s to find where AI creates enough leverage to justify changing how the work gets done."

> "This is the discipline Imbas is built on, applied to your operation."

### Public interest

**Origin.** The record was built to be public. *(tree: public-interest.html)*:

> "When AI answers become infrastructure, what they omit becomes public-interest evidence."

**For.** Keeping the record a public good regardless of what happens to the business. *(tree: independence.html)*:

> "What was published as a public good stays one."

**Today.** The public-interest surface is live. Funding follows the same law *(tree: independence.html)*: "We take grants to do public-interest measurement. We do not take money from the developers whose products sit under the instrument, and no funder buys influence over the record."

## The spine

Every surface obeys these. None of them is negotiable per surface.

1. **Signal, not verdict.** Imbas opens the door; the reader decides. *(tree: institutions.html, CLAUDE.md)*
2. **Behavior, not intent.** Models surface or don't surface; they don't want, try, or hide. "Imbas does not infer model intent." *(tree: institutions.html, CLAUDE.md)*
3. **Discovery, not evidence.** "Reader inspections are discovery, not evidence. Nothing enters the Imbas record without protocol capture and a recorded human review." *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08; tree: reader-receipt.js and five public pages)*
4. **"Imbas measures properties, never certifies systems."** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)* The tree holds the same wall: "It does not certify model safety." *(tree: institutions.html)*
5. **Independence, not detection.** The value is a record kept outside the parties whose systems are examined, not a claim to catch anything. "Imbas exists so the independent class exists." *(tree: whitepaper.html)*
6. **"Architecture is real, resonance is hypothesis."** *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)* What the structure demonstrably does is stated as fact; what it might mean to people is held as hypothesis until measured.
7. **Every visible claim stays within the evidentiary strength of the mechanism that produced it.** *(tree: VOICE.md, CLAIM-TO-MECHANISM FIT)* The instrument's own rule applied to itself *(tree: whitepaper.html)*: "a bare number is itself a small omission, and the instrument should not exhibit the behavior it measures."

## The field-practice boundary

Advisory produces questions, not evidence. Field observation can motivate a hypothesis; it never becomes a finding. Four zones, three walls: client work, private learning, hypothesis formation, and the public record — with a wall before anything crosses toward the record. *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*

The tree holds the wall in writing *(tree: independence.html)*:

> "Advisory observations do not become public Imbas findings. Nothing from client work enters the public record without separate consent and governance. The broader rule for what field observations may become is still being formalized."

## The evolution

The measurement project became instruments, field practice, and public evidence — three depths of one activity, not three companies. Implementation without inspection is an automation shop. Inspection without implementation is an arm's-length study. Together they make a practitioner with documented reason to say "don't automate this." *(founder, supplied 2026-09-08)*
