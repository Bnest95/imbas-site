How I work

Start with the people who know how the work gets done.

I talk to the people doing the work and look at the tools, information and decisions involved. We try useful changes on real tasks, work through the exceptions and make sure people understand how to use what we build.

Before we start, we agree what you’ll receive. Depending on the work, that can include findings and recommendations, a working tool or process, training and instructions for your team. We also agree who controls the accounts, who maintains what we build and what support is included.

Scope and fees depend on the problem. We work that out together after an initial conversation.
