What I take on

I research the options, build what’s needed, put it in place and train the people who’ll use it.

  • AI adoption

    Where AI can save your team time, where its work needs checking, and where it isn’t worth using yet.

  • Custom tools

    A quoting tool built around your services and prices, a searchable reference from your own documents, reports prepared from the records you already keep, or customer inquiries and follow-ups in one place, each with a next step and someone responsible for it.

  • Implementation

    Putting the new tool or process into daily use, connected to the software and records you already have.

  • Training

    Training using your team’s own tasks, so people know how to use the tools, check the results and handle exceptions.

  • Research

    A comparison of your options, backed by research into the market, suppliers or competitors.

  • Customer research

    Conversations with your customers and a review of their questions and feedback, so you know why they choose you and why some don’t.

  • Positioning

    A clearer offer, based on what customers value and what stops them from buying.

How I work

Start with the people who know how the work gets done.

I talk to the people doing the work and look at the tools, information and decisions involved. We try useful changes on real tasks, work through the exceptions and make sure people understand how to use what we build.

Before we start, we agree what you’ll receive. Depending on the work, that can include findings and recommendations, a working tool or process, training and instructions for your team. We also agree who controls the accounts, who maintains what we build and what support is included.

Scope and fees depend on the problem. We work that out together after an initial conversation.

Tell me what’s slowing you down.
